## Verification checklist

- Confirm the official or authoritative source and exact citation.
- Confirm the version in effect on the legally relevant date, including amendments and effective dates.
- For cases, confirm court, date, subsequent history, precedential status, and whether binding or persuasive.
- Confirm applicability to the specific court, claim, issue, and procedural posture.
- Record contrary, superseding, or distinguishing authority.
- Cite the authority precisely in legal analysis and retain a source copy or stable link where permitted.
- Obtain human review before relying on a legal conclusion in a filing, hearing, or negotiation.
